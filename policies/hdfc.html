📊 FOIR (Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio)

Monthly Salary Maximum FOIR
₹25,000 – ₹50,000 Up to 60%
₹50,001 – ₹1,00,000 Up to 65%
Above ₹1,00,000 Up to 70%
Simple Explanation

FOIR ka matlab hai customer ki salary ka kitna hissa EMI me ja sakta hai.

Example:
Salary ₹50,000 hai aur FOIR 60% hai.
Maximum total EMI ≈ ₹30,000 tak consider ki ja sakti hai.

📈 Multiplier Concept

Loan amount customer ki income, FOIR, company category aur bank policy ke hisaab se calculate hota hai.

Higher salary + achha CIBIL + achhi company category = generally higher loan eligibility.
